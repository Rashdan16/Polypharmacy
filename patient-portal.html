ℹ️ Understanding your risk score

Your risk score is calculated by your clinical team across six areas: Fall Risk, Bleeding Risk, Anticholinergic Burden (ACB), QT Prolongation Risk, Frailty, and Renal Risk. A higher score indicates a greater need for a medication review.

This score does not mean you are in danger. It helps your pharmacist and GP identify which medications may need to be reviewed together. Do not stop or change any medication without speaking to your clinical team first.
